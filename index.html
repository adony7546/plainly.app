Total revenue fell from $14,250 to $12,540 compared to last week.

What Happened

Traffic actually increased 35%, but overall conversion rate fell from 2.8% to 1.9%.

Why It Might Have Happened

Most new visitors came on mobile, and mobile conversion fell the most. Mobile checkout may be losing people, or the new visitors may be less ready to buy. These are leads to check, not confirmed causes.

🔍 Recommended Actions to Investigate

  • 1. Check mobile product pages and checkout experience on smartphones to identify potential friction points.
  • 2. Review traffic sources to see if incoming visitor intent aligns with your product offerings.

Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Is my store data safe?

Yes. Plainly connects using read-only API access. We only ask for read-only access and use it to build your report.

Do you change anything in my store?

Never. Because our connection is strictly read-only, Plainly cannot make any edits, alter products, modify themes, or touch orders. We only read analytics data to generate your insights report.

Which e-commerce platforms are supported?

We currently support Shopify and WooCommerce stores. Additional platform integrations are coming soon based on user requests.

How much does Plainly cost?

Plainly is completely free for early access users during our preview period. We will introduce straightforward tiered pricing later, but early supporters will keep special perks.

Is the AI always right?

Insights are suggestions to investigate, not guaranteed causes. E-commerce has many variables, and Plainly highlights anomalies and likely drivers to help guide your investigation efficiently.
